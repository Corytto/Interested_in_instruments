THE CRAFTSMAN

Built by hand. Built to last.

At Interested in Instruments, every guitar is treated as an individual instrument rather than a product rolling off an assembly line.

From selecting the right tonewoods to shaping the neck, finishing the body, and dialing in the final setup, every step is approached with patience and attention to detail.

The goal is simple: build an instrument that feels natural in your hands and sounds uniquely yours.
